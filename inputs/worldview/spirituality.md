# Spirituality & The Internal Struggle

> Derived from: MY DIARY / MY THOUGHTS / WORKS (Notion Entry: 24/02/26)

## Core Concepts

### 1. The True Nature of the Devil (Iblees)
The Devil is not an external, haunting, or superstitious entity. It is an **Influencer**. It does not initiate the evil thoughts; it merely reinforces them.

### 2. The Internal Landscape: Nafs and the Id
- **Nafs al-Ammorah (The Id):** The primal, subconscious component of the human mind that pushes toward desires and commits sins. It is the monstrous, internal part that needs no external prompting to sway us.
- **Nafs al-Mutmainna (The Superego):** The positively reinforcing energy. It clearly knows what is right versus what is wrong and actively cancels out the selfish, disastrous impulses of the Nafs al-Ammorah. 

### 3. The Battle of Reinforcements
- The **Devil** reinforces the *Nafs al-Ammorah*.
- The **Fear of God** acts as the crucial reinforcement for the *Nafs al-Mutmainna*.
- When the Nafs al-Ammorah overrides the Nafs al-Mutmainna, sins occur. 
- *Insight:* The presence of both opposing forces continuously battling and cancelling each other out is the reason why there is generally more good than evil in the world.

### 4. The Purpose of Sin
Sins happen when the internal balance is lost, but they serve a profound secondary purpose: **they bring us closer to God**. By failing, a human realizes their flaw and turns back to God for forgiveness and strength to reinforce the Nafs al-Mutmainna. It is a necessary friction for spiritual growth.
