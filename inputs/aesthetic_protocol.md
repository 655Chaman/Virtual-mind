# Aesthetic Mastery Weekly Protocol

To build that elite aesthetic while managing your AI agency and engineering studies, your week must be structured for maximum efficiency. Since you’re 6'1", we are prioritizing **posture, width, and metabolic health.**

## The Weekly Schedule

| Day | Focus | Location | Goal |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Monday** | **The Foundation (Chest/Back)** | **Gym** | Heavy compounds for the "V-Taper." |
| **Tuesday** | **Aesthetic Volume (Shoulders/Core)** | **Home** | Width and midsection tightness with 12kg DB. |
| **Wednesday**| **Lower Body Power & Posture** | **Gym** | Legs and Mid-back thickness. |

## Daily Execution Blueprint

### Monday: The Foundation (Gym)
* **Morning:** 500ml water + 20 mins of "Deep Work" for Elesium.online before distractions.
* **Workout:**
    1.  **Incline Dumbbell Press:** 3 sets x 8–10 reps (Focus on the stretch at the bottom).
    2.  **Lat Pulldowns (Wide Grip):** 3 sets x 10–12 reps (Drive elbows down).
    3.  **Seated Cable Rows:** 3 sets x 10–12 reps (Squeeze shoulder blades).
    4.  **Dumbbell Lateral Raises:** 4 sets x 15 reps (Keep it light, focus on form).
* **Nutrition:** Largest carb meal *after* this workout. **Protein Anchor:** 150g Chicken/Paneer or 5 Egg Whites.

### Tuesday: Aesthetic Volume (Home)
* **Morning:** Fasted 15-min walk to clear the "engineering brain."
* **Workout (12kg DB):**
    1.  **Decline Push-ups (Feet on Bed):** 4 sets to failure.
    2.  **Leaning Lateral Raises:** 5 sets x 15 reps (3-1-1 Tempo).
    3.  **Single-Arm DB Row:** 3 sets x 12 reps (Use a table for support).
    4.  **Plank:** 3 sets x 60 seconds.
* **Night:** Magnesium L-Threonate (1 hour before bed) + No blue light.

### Wednesday: Lower Body & Posture (Gym)
* **Workout:**
    1.  **Leg Press:** 3 sets x 12–15 reps (Full range of motion).
    2.  **Seated Overhead DB Press:** 3 sets x 10 reps.
    3.  **Face Pulls:** 3 sets x 15 reps (Crucial for 6'1" posture).
    4.  **Leg Curls/Extensions:** 3 sets x 12 reps.
* **Nutrition:** High protein focus. If the day is busy with sales calls, use a Whey shake to stay on track.

### Thursday: Active Recovery
* **Focus:** Mental dominance and recovery.
* **Movement:** Hit **10,000 steps**. This is your primary fat-burning tool for the "skinny-fat" fix.
* **Nutrition:** Lower carbs today since there is no heavy lifting. Double the fiber (salads/veggies).

### Friday: Upper Body Hypertrophy (Gym)
* **Workout:**
    1.  **Flat Dumbbell Press:** 3 sets x 10 reps.
    2.  **Pull-ups (or Assisted):** 3 sets to failure (Focus on the "hang" at the bottom).
    3.  **Chest-Supported Rows:** 3 sets x 12 reps.
    4.  **Dips:** 3 sets to failure (Lean forward to hit the chest).
* **Night:** 2000mg Magnesium L-Threonate. This is a big work night for your agency—the magnesium will help you "switch off" after high-intensity brain work.

### Saturday: Detail & Finishing (Home)
* **Workout (12kg DB):**
    1.  **Bulgarian Split Squats:** 3 sets x 10 per leg (Brutal but will fix skinny legs).
    2.  **Standing Lateral Raises:** 5 sets x 20 reps (Burnout sets).
    3.  **Overhead DB Extension:** 3 sets x 15 reps (Targeting the long head of the tricep).
    4.  **DB Curls:** 3 sets x 12 reps (3-second eccentric).

## Daily Bio-Rules

### 1. The "Protein Anchor" (Every Meal)
For every carb-heavy Indian meal, you must consume your protein source **first**.
* **Target:** 160g–175g Protein daily.
* **Budget Hack:** 6 Egg whites + 100g Soya chunks = ~50g Protein for very low cost.

### 2. The 3-1-1 Tempo
Since your home weight is capped at 12kg, you must use this for every rep:
* **3 Seconds:** Lowering the weight (The eccentric).
* **1 Second:** Pause at the peak stretch.
* **1 Second:** Explosive contraction.

### 3. The Magnesium Protocol
Take your **Magnesium L-Threonate** exactly **60 minutes before your head hits the pillow**. 
* If you take it and then scroll on your phone, you are wasting the cognitive benefits. 
* Use that 60-minute window for "low-arousal" tasks: reading, planning the next day's agency outreach, or stretching.

### 4. Step Tracking
As a 6'1" guy, you have a high "Non-Exercise Activity Thermogenesis" (NEAT) potential. If you don't hit your **10k steps**, you will struggle to lose the "skinny-fat" midsection regardless of how hard you lift.
