## *THE 3 PILLARS TO BUILD SIMULTANEOUSLY*

### *PILLAR 1: ISLAMIC KNOWLEDGE (The Foundation You Cannot Skip)*

*You cannot represent Islam without knowing Islam at a deep level. Here's the study track:*

***Quarter 1 (Now → May 2026): Aqeedah (Creed) + Seerah***

- *Book: "Kitab At-Tawheed" by Muhammad ibn Abdul Wahhab — understand the essence of what you're calling people to*
- *Book: "The Sealed Nectar" (Ar-Raheeq Al-Makhtum) by Mubarakpuri — the Prophet's ﷺ strategy was political, military, economic, and spiritual. Study it as a BLUEPRINT, not just a biography*
- *Online: Yaqeen Institute articles for modern Islamic intellectual discourse*

***Quarter 2 (Jun → Aug 2026): Fiqh + Islamic History***

- *Study how the Rashidun Caliphate actually functioned — its governance, its economics, its judicial system*
- *Book: "The Great Theft" by Khaled Abou El Fadl — understand the internal debates within Islam honestly*
- *Study the Ottoman Caliphate's administrative system — what worked, what failed*

***Quarter 3 (Sep → Nov 2026): Rhetoric + Da'wah Methodology***

- *Study the Quran's own argumentation style — how does Allah construct arguments?*
- *Book: "Reclaim Your Heart" by Yasmin Mogahed — understand how to speak to hearts, not just minds*
- *Watch and study Ahmed Deedat, Zakir Naik, Hamza Yusuf, Mohammed Hijab — different styles, different audiences. Learn from all of them.*

***Quarter 4 (Dec 2026 → Feb 2027): Arabic + Tafsir Deep Dive***

- *By now you should be reading basic Arabic. Push harder.*
- *Start studying Tafsir of key Surahs: Al-Baqarah, Al-Imran, An-Nisa — the political and social Surahs*
