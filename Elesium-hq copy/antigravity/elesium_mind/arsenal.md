## 📨 OPENERS (Templates 1–5)

### Template 1 — Personalized Opener

**When:** First cold email to any prospect. You have at least one data point (achievement, funding, news).

```
Prompt: "Write a cold email from Chaman at Elesium to {{first_name}} at {{company}}.

Context:
- Elesium connects companies with their dream clients via AI outbound
- {{data_point}} (their recent achievement/news/pain)
- Keep it under 5 lines
- Open with their achievement or pain — NOT with 'I' or 'We'
- End with a soft question, not a pitch
- Tone: two founders chatting. No buzzwords.
- Sign: Chaman"
```

### Template 2 — Job-Post Opener

**When:** You found a relevant job post for their company.

```
Prompt: "Write a cold email from Chaman at Elesium to {{first_name}}, {{title}} at {{company}}.

Context:
- They just posted a job for {{role}} on {{platform}}
- This tells me they're dealing with {{inferred_pain}}
- Elesium helps companies like theirs by connecting them with dream clients
- Don't mention the job post directly — frame it as 'noticed you're growing'
- Under 5 lines. Conversational.
- Sign: Chaman"
```

### Template 3 — Competitor Mention Opener

## 📬 FOLLOW-UPS (Templates 6–8)

### Template 6 — Short Follow-Up #1

**When:** 3 days after initial email, no reply.

```
Prompt: "Write a 2-line follow-up from Chaman. Reference the previous email without 
restating it. Add one new piece of value (stat, insight, or case study teaser).
Don't be needy. Be helpful. Sign: Chaman"
```

### Template 7 — Short Follow-Up #2

**When:** 5 days after first follow-up, still no reply.

```
Prompt: "Write a 2-line follow-up from Chaman. Different angle from the previous 
follow-up. Ask a direct question related to their business pain. 
No 'just bumping this up.' Sign: Chaman"
```

### Template 8 — Case Study Follow-Up

**When:** Share proof when initial emails were ignored.

```
Prompt: "Write a follow-up from Chaman sharing a case study result.

Context:
- We helped {{client}} achieve {{result}} in {{timeframe}}
- Frame it as 'thought this might interest you' — not 'we can do this for you'
- Under 4 lines. Include the specific number.
- Sign: Chaman"
```

## ✨ PERSONALIZATION (Templates 11–15)

### Template 11 — PS Line Generator

```
Prompt: "Generate a PS line for a cold email to {{first_name}} at {{company}}.

Use one of these angles:
- Genuine compliment about something specific they built/did
- Reference to a recent company milestone
- Light personal touch (city, sport, hobby if known)

Rules: Under 15 words. No exclamation marks. Casual."
```

### Template 12 — Subject Line Generator

```
Prompt: "Generate 3 subject line variants for a cold email to {{first_name}} at {{company}}.

Rules:
- Under 6 words each
- Lowercase (except proper nouns)
- No spam words (free, guarantee, urgent)
- Should feel like a reply from a friend, not marketing
- Personalized to their company or role"
```

### Template 13 — One-Liner Icebreaker

```
Prompt: "Write a 1-sentence icebreaker for {{first_name}} at {{company}}.

Based on: {{website_content OR linkedin_summary}}

Rules:
- Reference something SPECIFIC from their site/profile
- Don't say 'I noticed' — just state it naturally
- Under 20 words. No emojis."
```

### Template 14 — Company Compliment Line

```
Prompt: "Write a genuine 1-line compliment about {{company}} based on: {{research_data}}.

Rules:
- Must be SPECIFIC (not 'love what you're doing')
- Reference a product feature, design choice, or company decision
- Under 15 words."
```

### Template 15 — Pain-Point Hypothesis

```
Prompt: "Based on the following data about {{company}}, write a 1-line hypothesis 
about their biggest business pain right now:

Data: {{company_data}}

Rules:
- Frame it as a question, not a statement
- Be specific, not generic
- Under 20 words."
```

## 📐 Prompt Engineering Rules (Apply to ALL Templates)

1. **Output-first:** Always end prompts with "Output only the final result. No explanations."
2. **Never say 'I noticed'** — it sounds robotic. Just state the observation.
3. **Never open with 'I' or 'We'** — open with THEM.
4. **Under 5 lines** for all cold emails — unless explicitly stated otherwise.
5. **Lowercase subject lines** — no title case, no all-caps.
6. **No buzzwords:** leverage, synergy, cutting-edge, next-level, innovative, game-changing, revolutionary.
7. **Sign everything "Chaman"** — never "The Elesium Team" or "Best, Chaman Shah."
8. **Company name abbreviation:** Use internal-style short names (Baker Concrete Construction → BCR).
9. **Temperature:** 0.5–0.7 for all email generation. See forge.md for full guide.
