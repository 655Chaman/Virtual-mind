# 🧠 The Pain-Sniffing Method

> **Core Philosophy**: Target leads based on the specific business pains you can solve, rather than just their job title and company.

---

## ❌ Instead of:
- Targeting all marketing directors at 500+ employee tech companies
- "Let's target companies showing signs they need better website copy"

## ✅ Do this:
- If your client runs a marketing agency offering web copywriting: look for companies struggling with poor website conversions due to bad copy
- You won't have exact data, but you get close

---

## 🎯 Three Core Tactics

### Tactic #1: Tech Stack
- If your client is a SaaS company, find businesses using similar software and offer them a better alternative
- Target SaaS companies using similar software and say: "I can connect you with dream clients already using similar software—by leveraging yours"

### Tactic #2: Job Posting (Primary Workflow)
- When a company is hiring for a specific role, it means they have a pain point you can solve
- **Copywriter** → they might need help improving their website copy
- **Sales Development Rep (SDR)** → they likely want more leads and sales opportunities
- Instead of waiting for them to fill the role, reach out and offer a solution right away

### Tactic #3: Funding Announcements
- Companies that recently raised funding often have money to spend and growth goals
- Series A funding → they likely need marketing, hiring, or sales support
- New round of investment → they may be looking for ways to scale
